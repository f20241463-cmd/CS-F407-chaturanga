Pieces

♚︎Raja (king): one square in any direction ♛︎Mantri (counselor): one square diagonally ♜︎Ratha (chariot): any distance in a straight line, like a rook ♝︎Gaja (elephant): jumps exactly two squares diagonally ♞︎Ashva (horse): an L-shaped jump, like a knight ♟︎Padati (foot soldier): one step forward, captures diagonally, becomes a Mantri on the last rank

Checkmate wins. Stalemate, threefold repetition and 100 moves without a capture or soldier move are draws. There is no castling, no double pawn step and no en passant.
